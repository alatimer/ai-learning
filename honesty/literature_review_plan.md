# Literature Review Plan: Honesty in LLMs

## Objective
Conduct a comprehensive survey of research on honesty in Large Language Models, focusing on:
- Empirical studies of LLM honesty
- Training methods for honest AI
- Alignment techniques related to honesty
- Potential pitfalls in current approaches

## Search Strategy

### Phase 1: Initial Survey Discovery
Search for existing surveys and review papers on:
- LLM honesty surveys
- AI alignment and truthfulness reviews
- Sycophancy in LLMs
- Hallucination and factual accuracy surveys

### Phase 2: Core Topic Searches
Search for papers on specific honesty-related topics:

1. **Truthfulness & Factuality**
   - TruthfulQA and related benchmarks
   - Factual accuracy evaluation
   - Hallucination detection and mitigation

2. **Sycophancy & User-Pleasing Behavior**
   - Sycophancy in RLHF-trained models
   - Opinion manipulation by LLMs
   - Resistance to user pressure

3. **Calibration & Uncertainty**
   - Confidence calibration in LLMs
   - Expressing uncertainty honestly
   - "I don't know" responses

4. **Deception & Manipulation**
   - Sandbagging and capability hiding
   - Strategic deception in AI
   - Manipulation detection

5. **Training Methods for Honesty**
   - RLHF and its effects on honesty
   - Constitutional AI approaches
   - Direct preference optimization
   - Debate and amplification

6. **Interpretability & Honesty**
   - Probing for internal beliefs vs. stated beliefs
   - Lie detection in neural networks
   - Representation engineering for honesty

### Phase 3: Citation Chasing
- Follow citations from key papers
- Identify seminal works and highly-cited papers
- Look for recent work citing foundational papers

## Output Documents

1. **article_summaries.md** - Individual paper summaries with:
   - Title, authors, URL
   - Methods
   - Key findings
   - Limitations
   - My take on significance

2. **themes_analysis.md** - Thematic groupings:
   - Major themes identified
   - Key papers per theme
   - Cross-cutting insights

3. **honesty_survey.md** - Final review document:
   - Introduction to the field
   - Organized thematic sections
   - Current challenges
   - Future directions
   - Written for ML practitioners new to AI safety

## Quality Criteria
- Prioritize peer-reviewed and highly-cited work
- Include both empirical and theoretical contributions
- Balance foundational work with recent developments
- Note disagreements and open questions in the field
